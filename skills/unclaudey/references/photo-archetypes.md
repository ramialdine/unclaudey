# Photographic directions

Starting points, not templates. Pick one because it fits the subject, then make it specific: add the subject's own materials, places and people to every brief. Each entry gives:
- **query modifiers**: words to add to briefs
- **pairs with**: type and layout that let the photos lead
- **watch for**: how the direction goes wrong

Don't default to the same two directions every time. If you used one on a recent project, choose differently unless the brief insists.

### 1. Documentary warmth
Real moments, natural light, people doing the work.
- **Query modifiers:** candid, window light, 35mm, shallow depth of field, working hands, unposed
- **Pairs with:** a humanist sans or a warm text serif; generous margins; full-bleed hero with text in the calm side
- **Watch for:** faces looking at the lens, or orange over-grading
- **Fits:** craft studios, restaurants, local services, nonprofits

### 2. Editorial still life
Objects as subjects: studio set, colored backdrop, hard shadows.
- **Query modifiers:** still life, hard shadow, colored paper backdrop, overhead or 45°, minimal props
- **Pairs with:** a high-contrast display face; asymmetric grids; product-as-hero
- **Watch for:** the laptop + coffee + succulent flat-lay cliché
- **Fits:** consumer products, cosmetics, publishing, food brands

### 3. Architectural minimal
Geometry, planes, repetition, negative space.
- **Query modifiers:** concrete, facade, stairwell, overcast, symmetry, looking up, clean lines
- **Pairs with:** a grotesk or geometric sans; strict grid; lots of whitespace; small type
- **Watch for:** generic glass office towers
- **Fits:** architecture, real estate, B2B with a strong brand, studios

### 4. Cinematic night
Neon, rain, sodium lamps, long exposure.
- **Query modifiers:** night, neon reflections, wet street, bokeh, blue hour, long exposure
- **Pairs with:** a dark theme; condensed display type; a single saturated accent taken from the photos
- **Watch for:** the near-black + acid-green generated look. Take the accent from the photo's real light instead.
- **Fits:** nightlife, music, gaming, film, late-night food

### 5. High-key airy
Bright, soft daylight, lots of white, light shadows.
- **Query modifiers:** bright, soft daylight, white walls, airy, pastel, overexposed slightly
- **Pairs with:** a light theme with a tinted (not pure) white background; a delicate serif or rounded sans
- **Watch for:** looking like a generic wellness app
- **Fits:** health, bridal, skincare, kids' products, clinics

### 6. Chiaroscuro
A single light source, deep shadows, a subject carved out of the dark.
- **Query modifiers:** dark background, single light source, low key, dramatic shadow, black backdrop
- **Pairs with:** a dark theme; serif display; slow reveals
- **Watch for:** muddy mid-tones on cheap screens. Check the contrast of text regions.
- **Fits:** spirits, coffee, luxury, portraiture, theater

### 7. Botanical and organic
Plants, leaves, macro textures, dew, soil.
- **Query modifiers:** macro, leaf veins, morning dew, moss, greenhouse, dappled light
- **Pairs with:** earthy palettes from the photos; soft serif; organic shapes used sparingly
- **Watch for:** "eco brand green" clichés
- **Fits:** gardening, tea, natural products, climate work

### 8. Aerial scale
Landscapes, patterns from above, tiny figures in big space.
- **Query modifiers:** aerial, drone, top-down, vast, tiny figure, patterns in landscape
- **Pairs with:** oversized type; wide layouts; sparse copy
- **Watch for:** wallpaper-style mountain vistas with no link to the subject
- **Fits:** travel, outdoors, logistics, energy, maps

### 9. Motion and energy
Movement, blur, sweat, spray, the peak of an action.
- **Query modifiers:** motion blur, mid-air, action shot, spray, freeze motion, panning
- **Pairs with:** condensed or italic display; diagonal crops; tight rhythm
- **Watch for:** generic gym stock
- **Fits:** sports, dance, events, youth brands

### 10. Material close-ups
Texture as the image: paper fibers, stone, linen, wood, water, rust.
- **Query modifiers:** texture, close-up, surface, grain, fibers, macro, flat light
- **Pairs with:** anything. Use as section backgrounds or bands with text on top (calm textures give copy space for free).
- **Watch for:** textures that fight the text. Use `tone` and `copy_space: center`.
- **Fits:** almost everything, including dev tools and SaaS where people photos feel fake

### 11. Film nostalgia
Grain, faded blacks, warm highlights, analog color.
- **Query modifiers:** film photography, 35mm film, grain, faded, vintage color, analog
- **Pairs with:** a grotesk or typewriter-adjacent face; off-white background; imperfect edges
- **Watch for:** faux-retro without purpose
- **Fits:** fashion, music, cafés, independent brands

### 12. Urban color
Painted walls, saturated street color, strong graphic shadows.
- **Query modifiers:** colorful wall, street, hard sunlight, graphic shadows, saturated
- **Pairs with:** a bold sans; color-blocked layouts using hues sampled from the photos
- **Watch for:** Instagram-wall cliché, the same pink wall everyone uses
- **Fits:** youth brands, festivals, food trucks, creative agencies

### 13. Monochrome reportage
Black-and-white, grain, real events.
- **Query modifiers:** black and white, reportage, grain, street photography, candid
- **Pairs with:** a newspaper-adjacent but *not* broadsheet-cliché layout. Avoid hairline-rule overload.
- **Watch for:** mixing B&W with color photos without a system
- **Fits:** journalism, nonprofits, history, archives, photographers

### 14. Food: dark rustic vs bright overhead
Choose one per site.
- **Dark rustic query modifiers:** dark wood, side light, steam, crumbs, 45°
- **Bright overhead query modifiers:** overhead, white table, daylight, colorful, hands reaching in
- **Watch for:** perfectly styled plates nobody would eat. Look for real texture and mess.

### 15. Technology without clichés
The physical side of technology: hardware details, cables, hands on real objects, workshop benches, the place the product is used.
- **Query modifiers:** close-up hardware, circuit, workshop, hands soldering, server cable detail, field work
- **Pairs with:** a mono or grotesk only if it fits the product; real product UI as the other half of the visuals
- **Watch for:** people pointing at screens, blue holograms, glowing brains
